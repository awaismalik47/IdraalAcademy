Who We Are

Spreading the Light of Quran, One Student at a Time

Idraak Quran Academy was founded with a simple belief: every Muslim, anywhere in the world, deserves access to authentic, high-quality Quran education from the comfort of their own home.

We connect students of all ages with qualified, certified male and female teachers for one-on-one live online classes — covering everything from the very first Arabic letter to complete memorization and Tafseer.

Today, families across the world trust Idraak to guide their children — and themselves — on a structured, supportive path to reading, understanding and memorizing the Holy Quran.
