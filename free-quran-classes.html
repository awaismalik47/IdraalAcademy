Quran Education Shouldn't Depend on Affordability

Through our Sadaqah Jariyah program, Idraak Quran Academy offers completely free Noorani Qaida and Quran Reading classes to students who cannot afford course fees — funded entirely by generous donors. Every seat is taught with the same care and quality as our paid courses.

1. Apply for a Free Seat

Fill out the short eligibility form with your details.

2. We Review Your Request

Our team verifies eligibility within 2-3 working days.

3. Get Matched with a Teacher

Approved students are paired with a qualified teacher.

4. Start Learning for Free

Attend regular one-on-one classes at no cost, for as long as needed.

Seats are limited and reviewed on a first-come, first-served basis.
