Course 01

Noorani Qaida

The foundation course for every student. Noorani Qaida teaches Arabic letter recognition, correct pronunciation (Makharij), joining letters into words, and basic Tajweed signs — building the skills needed before reading the Quran itself.

  • Arabic alphabet & correct pronunciation (Makharij)
  • Joining letters and reading short words
  • Introduction to Tajweed symbols
  • Ideal for beginners of all ages

Course 02

Quran Reading

Once the fundamentals are solid, students move into reading the Holy Quran itself, page by page, with an emphasis on fluency, correct pronunciation, and applying Tajweed rules in real recitation.

  • Fluent Quran recitation from Mushaf
  • Applied Tajweed while reading
  • Correction of common recitation mistakes
  • Weekly progress tracking

Course 03

Quran Memorization (Hifz)

Our Hifz program uses proven repetition and revision techniques (Sabaq, Sabqi, Manzil) to help students memorize the Quran at a comfortable, sustainable pace while keeping earlier memorized portions strong.

  • Structured daily memorization targets
  • Regular revision (Sabqi & Manzil) system
  • One-on-one listening & correction
  • Certificate on Hifz completion

Course 04

Tajweed Rules

A focused course in the science of Tajweed for students who already read Quran but want to correct and refine their recitation — covering Makharij, Sifaat, rules of Noon & Meem, Madd, and Waqf.

  • Makharij-ul-Huroof (articulation points)
  • Rules of Noon Sakinah & Tanween
  • Madd (elongation) rules & Waqf (stopping)
  • Practical recitation practice every class

Course 05

Quran Translation & Tafseer

Go beyond recitation to understand what Allah is saying. This course covers word-for-word translation and classical Tafseer, helping students connect the meaning of the Quran to daily life.

  • Word-by-word Quran translation
  • Classical Tafseer explanations
  • Context of revelation (Asbab al-Nuzul)
  • Suitable for teens & adults
